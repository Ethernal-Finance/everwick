A few things you might be wondering

Before you move in.

Can I play right now?

Yes. Create a free account with your email, account name and a password of at least 12 characters. You receive one Generation S starter citizen to begin Story, work, own a home or business, and explore dungeons. This is early software and progress may be reset during development.

Does the world really run when I’m away?

Yes. The simulation runs on the server, independent of your browser. If the local server is stopped, it catches up elapsed time when restarted. A town hour currently takes 15 real seconds.

Are all conversations powered by a language model?

Citizens can speak through a configured local language model, using their personality and remembered conversations. If the model is unavailable, labeled memory-based dialogue keeps conversations working. Routine life and economic actions follow the game simulation.

Do I need cryptocurrency?

No. The town runs on internal coins. There are no wallets, tokens, deposits or cash-outs. Everwick is a conventional game with an internal currency.

What am I buying as a founder?

Optional Generation A claim slots: Founder $25 (1 any sex), Breeder $49 (1 male + 1 female), Town $75 (2), City $199 (3), Patron $499 (5). A–Y can breed when household requirements are met, with a limit of 50 births per parent. Z is sterile. Free starters cannot breed or transfer. Test-mode purchases have no monetary value. See the current sales status above.

What do referrals unlock?

Planned rewards: a profile decoration for 3 referrals, a cosmetic for 10, and a rare title for 25. These are future game rewards. Referral counts are provisional until email verification and abuse review are available.

Where are the community and press links?

Official community accounts have not been announced. The development press kit is included with the project; it contains approved product descriptions and a checklist for final contact details and screenshots.
