# Fishing waters

Fishing is no longer tied to a single fixed dock coordinate.

A player may cast while standing beside any water tile in the world. The server checks nearby terrain before accepting the cast and records the water area with the line. Reeling from a different water area fails, so a cast cannot be started in one lake and collected somewhere else.

Willowbank keeps its familiar fishing dock and local stock. Silverfin Shoals near Bluewater Reach has a larger stock capacity, faster replenishment, and a 1.5 abundance factor that shortens expected bite time. Other wilderness water regions receive independent local stocks rather than drawing from Willowbank.

Fish sales still transfer real town currency. Preparing a catch still converts one stored fish into one food item.
