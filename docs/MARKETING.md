# Everwick launch system

## Positioning
**Life goes on. Even when you log off.**

Everwick is a small town of a hundred citizens with jobs, ambitions and long memories. Players join the neighbourhood, operate a business and return to discover how things changed. Sell curiosity about people and consequences; explain the technology only after the fantasy is clear.

Primary audience hypotheses: cozy simulation players, management-game players and emergent-story fans. Validate these segments independently. Do not call templated dialogue a language-model conversation in clips. Label scripted demonstrations, seeded tests and accelerated simulation footage clearly.

## Four phases

1. **Curiosity before alpha:** publish 3–4 short authentic clips/week. Capture a baseline, record the real event IDs, then show the result. CTA: join the development list.
2. **Founder campaign:** launch only after payment, terms and benefit gates pass. Pin the exact development scope and display settled funding totals. Publish weekly shipped/next/blocked updates. CTA: support development or join free updates.
3. **Closed alpha:** invite small cohorts, interview returning and departed players, and create consent-based story submissions. Players share Gazette editions and business milestones. CTA: tell a friend a true town story.
4. **Public launch:** coordinate trailer, creator previews, live demonstrations, press kit, community moderation and support coverage. Explore Steam only after platform requirements and build suitability are researched. Do not announce an unsupported release date.

## Clip template (25–40 seconds)

- 0–3s: one concrete hook from a real event: “Our tavern stopped paying wages.”
- 3–8s: establish the citizen and business; show the baseline cash/wage values.
- 8–22s: show the decision and accelerated aftermath; disclose the time compression.
- 22–32s: reveal the consequence using the ledger, memory or newspaper.
- 32–40s: “Would you hire them? Follow Everwick / join the development list.”

Capture checklist: event/tick IDs, before/after save, reproducible steps, no emails or private account data, subtitle file, clean game audio/music with its own license, and attribution where required. Never invent a dramatic outcome and imply it emerged in play.

## Ready-to-adapt post templates

**NPC memory:** “I tipped [citizen] [amount] coins on Day [x]. On Day [y], they remembered [actual memory]. A small act, a lasting opinion. Everwick development alpha.”

**Business story:** “[business] sold [actual units] yesterday. I changed [price/wage/hours]. Here’s what happened to [sales/staff/profit] over [days].”

**Supply chain:** “Ore → tools → farm output → your dinner. We interrupted [real supplier] in a test town and tracked [measured effect]. Test scenario, accelerated [factor]×.”

**Gazette:** “THE GLOB GAZETTE, DAY [day]: [actual headline]. [One sentence from the recorded event]. What would you do next?”

**Weekly dev report:** “Shipped: [3 concrete features]. Learned: [one observation with sample size]. Still broken: [one honest issue]. Next: [bounded commitment]. Funding: [verified gross, refunds, net, founder count].”

**Community discussion:** “We’re building a town where businesses pay wages and citizens remember their employers. Here is a real [scenario] from this week. What would make this decision interesting to you?” Follow each community's self-promotion rules; participate before linking.

## Founder campaign plan

- Four tiers at $25/$75/$199/$499; no equity, token, yield or financial-return language.
- Initial transparent milestone goal: $25,000; report test data separately and exclude refunds from progress.
- Seven-day preview: demonstrate current build, benefit statuses, roadmap and refund terms.
- 21-day campaign: publish two weekly progress posts, three genuine simulation clips/week and one live Q&A/week.
- Supporter promises must match available fulfillment capability. Keep Discord benefits conditional until connected. Do not sell economic dominance.
- Referral rewards: 3/10/25 confirmed referrals unlock profile decoration/cosmetic/title. Counts remain provisional until email verification and abuse checks are live.
- If funding is slower than expected, reduce scope and explain it. Do not fabricate scarcity, testimonials or momentum.

## Measurement
Track traffic source → waitlist → account → first conversation → first business → day-1/day-7 return → founder purchase. Review cohorts by source and signup week. Report denominators, exclude internal testing, reconcile revenue to Stripe, and annotate releases. Watch story shares per returning player alongside conversion. A popular clip is not proof of a sticky game.
